# Release notes

## v0.1.0 — 2026-10-23

First public release of Riemann Sheet RPG.

- Explore nine expeditions covering square roots, independent roots, logarithms, Chew–Mandelstam functions, and Breit–Wigner resonance amplitudes.
- Follow analytic continuation across straight and curved branch cuts, including finite and infinitely many sheets.
- Find keys and chests on other sheets using sheet and coordinate clues; earn awards for sheets entered by your character.
- Choose Player, Top, or Spot views and terrain based on the real part, imaginary part, or magnitude of the function.
- Get started with a guided tour, reference links, and an optional Easy mode that reveals distant objective markers on viewed sheets.
- Move with WASD or arrow keys, turn with Q/E, change terrain with T, and restart with R; mouse/touch navigation and on-screen controls are also available.
- Save discoveries and preferences locally in the browser.
- Released under the MIT license, with a static GitHub Pages deployment and 69 automated tests.

Play at https://mmikhasenko.github.io/riemann-sheet-rpg/.
